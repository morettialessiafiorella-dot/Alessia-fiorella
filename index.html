Why good books go unnoticed

Most authors don’t have a writing problem. They have a positioning problem.

The description tells, not sells

It explains the plot but not the experience. A browsing reader needs to know what they will feel and why this book is for them.

The wrong shelves

Wrong categories and keywords put the book where its ideal readers never look.

Mixed signals

The cover promises one genre and the story delivers another, so the right readers scroll past.

Scattered promotion

A few posts, one swap and one ad, with no plan connecting them and no way to see what worked.

Launch week only

The first weeks matter, but the months after decide whether the book keeps selling.

Slow reviews

Without early reviews, new readers hesitate. I plan for them from the start.
